# GiftWise v1: user testing guide

Goal: watch real people do the core task **without helping**, and write down what they did and said.

## Who
2–3 people. The people interviewed for the proposal are the best choice. Otherwise, anyone shopping for holiday gifts this year.

## Setup (1 min)
- Open https://giftwise-fawn.vercel.app on *their* phone or laptop.
- Say: "I'm testing my app, not you. Please think out loud. I won't help, so just do what you'd normally do."

## The task (read exactly this, nothing more)
> "Think of a real person you need to buy a holiday gift for this year. Use this site to find a gift for them that fits your budget, and decide where you'd buy it."

Stop when they say they've chosen something, or after 6 minutes.

## Follow-up questions (after the task)
1. Was there a moment you weren't sure what to do?
2. Did you trust the prices? Why or why not?
3. Would you actually buy any of these ideas?
4. What's the one thing you'd change?
5. Would knowing the price history (is this a good deal?) change your decision?
